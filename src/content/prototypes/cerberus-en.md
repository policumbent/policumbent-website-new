## History

Cerberus is Team Policumbent's first arm-powered prototype. It has been designed with the intention to break the world record of 83.02 km/h, Cerberus was capable of overcome this speed during the 2024 WHPSC, reaching 83.28 km/h and claiming the new world record.

During October 2025, Cerberus broke the Hour Record, travelling 34.90 km/h on the High Speed Track of the Balocco Proving Ground.

Fun fact: it has 3 wheels, just like the number of heads of the mythological beast it was named after.

## Features

- Sandwich carbon-PVC-kevlar front fairing
- Glass fiber rear fairing
- Sandwich carbon-PVC structural shell
- Carbon and aluminium reinforcement
- Rear wheel drive
- Rear hydraulic braking system (180mm rotor)
- 10 speed electronic moving cassette
- Double camera vision system with independent circuits
- Adjustable seat position
- Custom lenticular ETRTO 406-20 wheels
- Carbon fiber wheel covers
- Air filter
