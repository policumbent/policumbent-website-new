## History

Phoenix rises from the ashes of the old Taurus, aiming to ignite Nevada's ground, beating the actual World Record set at 144.19km/h.

In 2021, on Balocco's proving ground, with Andrea Gallo to the pedals, it touched the hour record, reaching speeds of up to 108 km/h.
As of now, after many technical adjustments, it is ready to battle on the World Human Powered Speed Challenge.

## Features

- Hull in carbon fiber-PVC-Kevlar sandwich composite carbon chassis with a thickness of about 2mm, covered with kevlar only in some points for safety
- 20" wheels
- Electronic vision system with double chamber and double screen electronic vision system with double chamber and double screen
- 11-speed front wheel gearbox with floating sprocket set
- 108T circular crown with anti-fall profile
- Single 203mm mechanical rear brake
