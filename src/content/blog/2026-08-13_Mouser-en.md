We welcome back Mouser in our family as Electronics Partner! Their support has been fundamental for the results we reached during these past three years, the World Speed, Hour and 10km Record, and we are thrilled to consolidate this partnership.

Mouser is a leading global distributor of electronic components, being a reference point for engineers and electronics designers. Thanks to Mouser, our Electronics Division will be able to use cutting edge electronic components for our on-board printed circuit boards, given also their efficiency in the supply chain.

_“Having Mouser on board again gives us the chance to experiment with increasingly sophisticated electronics on our prototypes, backed by fast and efficient logistics. These are key elements for developing reliable solutions and a great opportunity to sharpen our technical skills as designers.”_, said Simone Viola, outgoing Electronics Division Leader of Team Policumbent.

For more information about Mouser's University Sponsorship program, please visit this website - [https://eu.mouser.com/en/educationalsales/](https://eu.mouser.com/en/educationalsales/)
