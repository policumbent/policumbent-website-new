**Team Policumbent** is pleased to announce a new sponsorship with **ABC Tools,** a leading Italian company in the field of tooling, thus becoming the **Main Partner** for the remainder of the 2026 season.

This collaboration will guarantee the Team the best possible tools for carrying out laboratory activities, such as prototype assembly and maintenance, as well as trackside operations, along with support in selecting and using the equipment. Indeed, part of the performance stems from the correct vehicle tuning, and being able to rely on an established reality like ABC Tools will be extremely helpful in extracting as much as possible from each prototype.

**Chiara Megiovanni,** head of the Team's **Logistics and Supply Chain division,** explains: "For a team that designs and builds high-performance prototypes, the precision and reliability of the working tools are fundamental aspects. Having an absolute benchmark like ABC Tools by our side represents a significant quality leap for our work in the workshop and on the track."

**Paola Rossetto, ABC Tools Marketing Manager,** states: "We are very happy to start this collaboration with Team Policumbent of Politecnico di Torino, a reality that shares important values with ABC Tools such as innovation, competence, precision, and passion. Through this partnership, we want to offer a concrete contribution to the work of a group of young talents by making professional tools and our know-how available. Supporting projects like this means for us investing in the skills and ideas of the new generations, creating a bridge between the world of education and that of business. This is the meaning of our project 'ABC Tools supports talent', of which the Policumbent Team is now also a part."

We would like to renew our heartfelt thanks to ABC Tools for the willingness shown in recent months and for the trust placed in our project.
